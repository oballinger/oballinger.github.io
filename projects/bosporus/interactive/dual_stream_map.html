One MMSI, two transmitters — eleven sanctioned tankers at the Bosporus, August–September 2026

Two emitters share each of these MMSIs. One is the ship. The other is a box that broadcasts a position the ship is not at — parked on a coordinate no receiver could produce, or dead-reckoning a moving track with speed and course identical to the bit. Both lanes below run the whole record, so where they are filled in the same column the MMSI was in two places at once — including, for KHRIZOPRAZ and PIROP, every hour of a piloted Bosporus transit.
